Delete your KARAR account

An account is optional in KARAR. If you signed in with Apple or Google, you can delete the account in the app: Profile › Account › Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, sessions and progress backups from our server and revokes Apple's access.

If you no longer have the app, install KARAR again, sign in with the same Apple or Google account and delete it there. We do not store your email address, so we cannot find an account from an email alone. Questions: bsdigitalapp+karar@gmail.com.
