KARAR — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

KARAR is published by BUSATER YAZILIM ANONİM ŞİRKETİ ("BUSATER", "we", "us"). Questions about this policy: sametterme0@gmail.com or bsdigitalapp@gmail.com.

This policy covers the KARAR app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

In short

  • You can play KARAR without an account. Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we keep a small account and a backup of your progress on our server so you can continue on another device. You can delete the account in the app at any time.
  • Players without KARAR Pro see ads from Google AdMob. Ads are personalized only if you allow it.
  • We collect pseudonymous usage statistics (for example which chapters are played and which app version is used) to run and improve KARAR. You can turn them off in the app.
  • If you allow notifications, we tell you when an app update is available.
  • We do not sell personal data, and nothing you type to the characters is used for advertising.

What is saved on your device

KARAR keeps four things in the app's private storage on your device:

  1. Your story progress: the choices you make, where you are in the story, the replies you type to characters and the characters' answers, and the times you started and last played.
  2. Your settings: language, vibration, volume levels and your choice about usage statistics.
  3. Your KARAR Pro status: a short record that your subscription was last confirmed as active and until when, so the app keeps working briefly without a connection. It contains no payment or account details.
  4. An installation identifier: a random number created on your device. It is used for usage statistics and notifications as described below. It is not your advertising identifier.

Text you type. When you type a reply to a character, the app reads it on your device to choose how the story answers. It is saved with your progress so the conversation appears again when you return. It is sent to our server only as part of your progress backup, and only if you sign in.

Deleting it. Replaying from the beginning replaces your saved progress. Deleting the app removes the data on your device. If your device is set to back up apps (for example to iCloud or Google), your operating system may include this data in your own backup, handled by Apple or Google under your account settings.

Story content downloads

To deliver new chapters and corrections without an app update, KARAR downloads story files (text, images and sounds) from our content server at karar-content.bsdigitalapp.workers.dev, hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. The app only asks for files. It does not send your progress, the replies you type, your settings or any identifier.

Like any website, the server necessarily receives your device's IP address and basic technical details of the request (such as its time) in order to deliver the files. We use them only to deliver the files. We do not keep request logs.

To check for app updates, KARAR also asks Apple's public App Store lookup service whether a newer version exists. That request contains only KARAR's app identifier.

Optional account

You can sign in with Apple or with Google in Profile. Signing in is never required to play.

  • What we receive: a stable user identifier from Apple or Google. Google, and Apple if you choose to share it, also tell us your email address at sign-in; we use it only at that moment and do not store it. We never receive your password.
  • What we store: an account number, the identifier from Apple or Google, the date the account was created and last used, secure session records, and backups of your story progress (including the replies you type) with up to five earlier versions per story and the type of device that saved them. If you sign in with Apple, we also keep an encrypted Apple token so that we can revoke Apple's access when you delete your account.
  • Security: to limit abuse, our server temporarily keeps a keyed, irreversible hash of your IP address; it is deleted within an hour.
  • Deleting your account: Profile › Account › Delete account. It deletes your account, sessions and backups from our server immediately and revokes the Apple token. Your progress on the device stays unless you delete the app.

Usage statistics

KARAR records pseudonymous events so that we can see how the app is used, find problems and plan new chapters:

  • Events: for example app opened, screen viewed, chapter started or finished, choice made (which option, not any text you type), KARAR Pro screen shown or closed, subscription confirmed by the store, ad shown, update notification opened.
  • With each event: the installation identifier, the time, the app version and build, the platform and operating system version, the device model, the app language, the country or region estimated from the network connection (the IP address itself is not stored), and whether the app came from the App Store, TestFlight or a development build. If you are signed in, events can be linked to your account number so that one person on several devices is counted once.
  • Never included: your name, email address, the text you type, contacts, precise location or advertising identifier.
  • Retention: individual events are deleted after 13 months; only totals are kept after that.
  • Your choice: Profile › Usage statistics. When it is off, the app sends no events.

Advertising

Players without KARAR Pro see banners, a full-screen ad when the app opens or returns, full-screen ads between screens, and optional rewarded ads (watch an ad to use KARAR without ads for 10 minutes). Subscribers to KARAR Pro see no ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google receives your IP address, information about your device and the app, and your interactions with ads. Google uses your device's advertising identifier only with your permission:

  • iPhone and iPad: KARAR asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, ads are not personalized.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: Google's consent message asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown. You can change them later in Profile › Privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and explains how it uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send a notification when a new version of KARAR is available in the App Store or Google Play, and nothing else. To do this, our server stores a push token for your device, the platform, the app version and build, the app language (so the notification arrives in your language), whether the app came from the App Store or a test build, the installation identifier and which update notifications were already sent. Notifications are delivered through Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) and Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings; tokens that stop working, or that belong to an app not opened for 12 months, are deleted.

What KARAR does not do

  • It does not ask for your phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • The only permissions it may ask for are notifications and, on iPhone and iPad, tracking for ads. Both are optional.
  • It does not sell or rent personal data.
  • It does not use the text you type to characters for advertising, statistics or training.

About motion data on iPhone. The iOS app contains a system description for motion data because an animation library it uses includes motion-sensor support. KARAR never turns that support on and never asks for motion data.

Data handled by Apple and Google

When you download KARAR from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google processes data about your purchase and download under their own privacy policies. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple or Google may give us crash reports and statistics about app use that do not identify you. If you test KARAR through TestFlight, feedback and crash reports you choose to send are shared with us by Apple.

KARAR Pro. If you subscribe to KARAR Pro, the purchase is made through Apple's or Google's in-app purchase system and handled by them under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details, name or account information. The app checks on your device, with Apple or Google, whether your subscription is active; it does not send anything about your purchase to us. Only the fact that the store confirmed a subscription is recorded in usage statistics.

Where data is processed and why

Our server and content server run on Cloudflare, Inc. infrastructure, which can process data in data centers outside your country. Google (advertising) and Expo (notifications) may process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual safeguards.

We process data to provide the account and backups you ask for (contract), to keep the service secure and to understand and improve the app through usage statistics (our legitimate interests, which you can opt out of), and to show personalized ads and send notifications only with your consent.

Children

KARAR is a story app for teens and adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, object to its processing or ask for a copy. You can delete your account and backups yourself in the app, turn usage statistics off, change your ad choices and turn notifications off. Write to sametterme0@gmail.com or bsdigitalapp@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change how KARAR processes personal data, we update this policy before the version with that change is released and, where the law requires it, ask for your permission in the app first. The effective date above shows when this policy last changed.
